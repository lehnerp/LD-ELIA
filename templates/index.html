Add to the current case?

Add to case merges the new export into the existing investigation. Sign-ins already in the case are recognized and not duplicated, newer Entra values (such as risk state) are updated, and all analyst marks and decision history are kept.

Start a new case discards every imported sign-in, mark and decision. A backup is downloaded first.
